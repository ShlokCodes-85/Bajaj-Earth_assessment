Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Bajaj Earths Sheets Sync is a technical demonstration that lets users view and edit Google Sheets data through a web interface.

Information we access

When you choose to connect a Google account, the application requests permission to access Google Sheets and files selected by you through Google Picker. We use this access only to read, create, update, and delete rows in the spreadsheet you select or create.

How we use information

Google account authorization data is used only to provide the connected-sheet features. The application does not sell, advertise against, or share your Google data with third parties.

Storage and retention

For this demonstration, OAuth session information and credentials are held in temporary server memory to support your active session. They are not stored in a persistent database and are lost when the server restarts or is redeployed.

Security

OAuth client secrets and service-account credentials are kept on the backend and are not bundled into the frontend application. Do not share credentials outside the authorized configuration.

Third-party services

The application uses Google OAuth, Google Sheets API, Google Drive API, and Google Picker. Your use of those services is also subject to Google's policies and terms.

Your choices

You can stop using the connected-sheet features at any time and revoke access from your Google Account's third-party connections page.

Contact

For questions about this demonstration or this policy, contact the project owner through the Bajaj Earths technical-task submission channel.
